## Notes/Questions

- fup is exported after removing unmatched persons and group_all
- fup_all is also exported before matching with group_all renamed to group.
- The intermediate variables CAPE_DISC, OXA_DISC, BEV_DISC, disc_cap_weeks, disc_oxa_weeks, and disc_bev_weeks are used in calculations but are not retained in the exported FUP tables.
- *Alternative chemotherapy variables: `"SV_CETUXIMAB", "SV_FLUOROUACIL", "SV_FOLINICACID", "SV_GIMERACIL", "SV_NIVOLUMAB", "SV_OTERACIL", "SV_PANITUMUMAB", "SV_PEMBROLIZUMAB", "SV_TEGAFUR", "SV_TIPIRACIL", "SV_TRIFLURIDINE", "SV_IRINOTECAN"`
